AI Fluency

Not a domain module — a meta-skill that sits on top of all of them. Every technique here is gated on already knowing where the evidence lives, which is what the other nine modules are for. This one is about getting an AI to help you find it instead of confidently pointing you at the wrong thing.

Nothing on these pages calls a model. The prompt comparisons are worked examples with the responses written out; the exercise is to run them yourself, against whatever AI tool you actually use, and compare what comes back.
