Not every DHCP fault starts at this trailhead

An address conflict looks different: the client has a real address, and something else is answering ARP for it. That’s either a static IP sitting inside the dynamic pool, or a lease handed out again after a client failed to renew. Compare the server’s lease table against what’s actually answering on the wire. This page leans on Signal vs. Silence and Fail-Open vs. Fail-Closed without re-explaining them.
