Back to the Abstracted Networking Gear trailhead

A genuinely different failure mode from everything else on this platform so far: not "the system is confused about its own state," but "the system knows less than it could, on purpose, because a product designer decided that's the right tradeoff for the target user."

The tradeoff, stated plainly

Consumer- and prosumer-oriented gear trades diagnostic depth for a simpler UI. A clean dashboard with no alerts doesn't mean the device is healthy — it can just as easily mean the vendor never built alerting logic for whatever's actually wrong. The gap isn't a bug; it's a design decision made for a different audience than you.

A real case: a loop confined to specific VLANs

General loop detection is built to catch a network-wide broadcast storm. A loop that only exists within a subset of VLANs never presents that way, so it never fires — not because the detection is broken, but because it was never designed to catch this shape of problem.

The only route to actually finding a loop like this: watch the upstream switch's port/client mapping over time. A device confirmed present on the downstream switch shows up on port 5 — then a few minutes later on port 17 — then back on port 5 again, repeating. That flapping between two ports in the forwarding table is the loop's fingerprint, visible only through direct observation, never through any alert the switch raised.

This is behavioral/temporal observation in its clearest form: there's nothing to read here, only something to watch. Worth naming explicitly, because it's easy to forget it's an option at all when every other technique on this platform starts with "check the log" or "read the response."

Where AI genuinely helps here, and where it can't

Asked about symptoms like this, an AI tool can reasonably guess "probably a loop" fairly quickly — general diagnosis is cheap and doesn't need much context. But finding the loop requires visibility into live switch state that no outside tool has access to, AI included. The guess is real help; it isn't the investigation.
